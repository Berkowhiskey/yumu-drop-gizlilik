## In short

You can play Yumu Drop offline and without an account. Joining the TESTERS
leaderboard is optional. When you select **JOIN** (KATIL), the game creates
an anonymous account for you and your score is stored on a server abroad.
The game has no ads or analytics, and we do not sell your data.

## Information stored on your device

Your settings, mission and career progress, selected costume and theme,
and the details of your **10 best local runs** (score, device date, costume,
stage and best combo) stay on your device. The online leaderboard does
not send that historical list. If you join, the game also stores your
refresh token, nickname selection and highest score awaiting submission
on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing app data removes local
records. For the server account, follow the [deletion steps](#delete).

## When you join the TESTERS leaderboard

Use **Records → TESTERS (TESTERLAR) → JOIN (KATIL)** to connect to Supabase
over HTTPS. Your account uses a randomly generated unique identifier.
Account creation and leaderboard participation require no real name,
email, phone number or location. Nicknames come from built-in word lists.

- Account and session information supports sign-in, session renewal and
  service security. Supabase may process your IP address and technical
  client information when you connect. The game does not derive your
  location from your IP address.
- The game sends scores for eligible Arcade runs completed after joining,
  a random run ID, the season, the number of hits and the run duration.
  Hit count and duration are used only for range checks; they are not
  written to the leaderboard database.
- The server stores each account's best Arcade score, the times the score
  was achieved and last submitted, the run ID, season, Yumu number and
  nickname list selections. Score submissions and
  leaderboard access are tied to the session.
- Other leaderboard participants may receive your nickname, Yumu number,
  score, rank and the UTC day of the score. The leaderboard does not
  provide your account ID, session tokens or IP address to other players.

**STOP SHARING** (PAYLAŞIMI DURDUR) stops new score submissions. Use
**DELETE MY DATA** (VERİLERİMİ SİL) to remove the account and its score entry.

## Purposes and international transfers

We process information to provide the leaderboard, manage sessions,
protect the service from abuse and respond to data requests. After you
join, this information is collected through the requests the game sends.
We receive the information you provide in a support request by email and
use it to assess and respond to your request.

Supabase Inc. provides the leaderboard infrastructure. The project's
database is hosted in Stockholm, Sweden. Leaderboard and session data
sent to the service from Türkiye are processed abroad. This transfer takes
place only when you choose to join the TESTERS leaderboard, at a point where
this notice is available to you. The legal characterisation under Articles 5
and 9 of the Turkish KVKK will be settled with expert advice and added to
this text before production release.

## Retention

| Record | Retention and deletion |
| --- | --- |
| Active account, score and nickname | Until successful account deletion or the end-of-season cleanup. After the test season closes, these records are deleted within 30 days. |
| Sessions, refresh tokens and session IP / client information | Linked to the account and may remain until account deletion. Account deletion includes the associated session records. |
| API request logs (including IP addresses) | One day on the current Free plan. |
| Auth audit records (including IP addresses) | Not written to the project database. Kept only in the provider's log system; one hour on the current Free plan. |
| Database backups | The current Free plan includes no automatic provider database backups, and no separate backups are made. |
| Deletion and support requests (email) | The email is deleted once the request is resolved. The date, Yumu number and outcome of the request are kept as a record. |

If the plan or provider changes, these periods may change; this policy is
updated before such a change takes effect.

## Deleting your data

**In the game:** Use Records → TESTERS → **DELETE MY DATA**
(VERİLERİMİ SİL) and tap again to confirm. When deletion succeeds, the
anonymous account and its associated active score, nickname and session
records are removed. To remove local records and settings, clear the app's
data or uninstall the game.

**Outside the game:** [Send a Yumu Drop data deletion request](mailto:yeetsgames45@gmail.com?subject=Yumu%20Drop%20data%20deletion).
Include your Yumu number. Other players can see Yumu numbers, nicknames
and score entries, so those details alone do not establish ownership.
Requests are therefore handled under this rule:

- If the account has not connected to the server at all in the **7 days**
  before the request, it is treated as no longer used on any device. The
  account and its associated score, nickname and session records are
  deleted within 30 days of the request.
- If the account has connected in the last 7 days, it is still in use on a
  device. Deletion must then be done with **DELETE MY DATA** in the game on
  that device; the account is not deleted on request. If you can no longer
  reach that device, write to us and we will look for another way to verify
  your request together.

We will inform you of the outcome by email. Accounts that never submitted a
score have no Yumu number; they are deleted in the end-of-season cleanup.
Do not email session tokens or passwords.

## Children and parents

The game has no ads, analytics, chat or free-text fields. The online
leaderboard is optional, uses anonymous accounts and does not ask for a
real name, email, phone number or location. Parents and legal guardians
can send privacy questions or deletion requests to the contact address
above.

## Your rights and requests

Where applicable, you can contact us to ask whether your data is processed,
request information about processing and transfers, ask for correction or
deletion, or object to processing. The [Turkish Personal Data Protection
Authority](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/) explains the rights and application
routes under the KVKK. Where applicable, you may complain to the competent
data protection authority.
